📖 NEC 2023 Comprehensive Study Modules

Detailed breakdowns by category covering General Code, Grounding & Bonding, Raceways, Motors, and Theory.

Module 1: General Code Requirements (Articles 90 - 110)

Covers enforcement, purpose, definitions, and installation requirements. Pay close attention to working space requirements under 110.26 (30 inches wide, depth based on voltage to ground, headroom 6.5 feet), dedicated equipment space, and high-leg identification on 3-phase 4-wire delta systems (110.15 - Orange color marking on phase B).

Module 2: Grounding and Bonding (Article 250)

One of the most heavily weighted sections on Utah state exams. Review the grounding electrode system (250.50 - water pipe, metal frame, concrete-encased electrode/Ufer ground, ground ring), sizing the grounding electrode conductor using Table 250.66, and sizing equipment grounding conductors (EGCs) using Table 250.122 based on the rating of the overcurrent device.

Module 3: Wiring Methods and Raceways (Chapter 3)

Covers support and securing intervals for NM cable (334.30 - within 12 inches of box, every 4.5 feet), AC/MC cable, rigid metal conduit (RMC), EMT, and PVC. Review box fill calculations (314.16), pull and junction box sizing (314.28 - 6 times trade size for straight pulls, 8 times for angle pulls), and underground cover requirements (Table 300.5).

Module 4: Motors, Air Conditioning, and Transformers (Chapters 4 & 450)

Master the application of Table 430.250 for motor full-load currents, sizing branch-circuit short-circuit protection using Table 430.52 (inverse time breakers at 250%), disconnecting means sizing (115% of FLC under 430.110), and transformer overcurrent protection rules (Table 450.3(B) - 125% or 250%).

⚖️ Utah DOPL Electrical Licensing Requirements & Laws

Official guidelines for Utah licensure, apprenticeship hours, and continuing education.

1. Licensing Classifications & Experience Prerequisites

  • Apprentice Electrician: Must be registered with the Utah Division of Occupational and Professional Licensing (DOPL) while working under supervision.
  • Journeyman Electrician: Requires verified completion of a 4-year (8,000 hours) approved apprenticeship program, including 576 hours of classroom instruction, plus passing the Utah Journeyman exam.
  • Master Electrician: Requires 2 years of active licensed experience as a Journeyman Electrician and passing the Master Electrician examination.

2. Continuing Education Unit (CEU) Cycle

  • Renewal deadline is **November 30 of odd-numbered years**.
  • Requires **16 total hours** of approved CEUs, with a minimum of **12 hours in Core NEC code updates**.
