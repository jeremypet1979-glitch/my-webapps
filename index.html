NEC 2023 Comprehensive Study Modules

Key code articles and theory essential for Utah journeyman and master exams.

  • Art. 110.26: Working clearances in front of electrical equipment (30" wide, depth based on voltage to ground, 6'6" headroom).
  • Art. 210.19(A)(1) / Note 4: Branch circuit voltage drop recommendations (Max 3% branch, 5% total combined).
  • Art. 250.50 & Table 250.66: Grounding electrode systems and Grounding Electrode Conductor (GEC) sizing.
  • Art. 310.16: Conductor ampacity tables and $75^\circ ext{C}$ / $90^\circ ext{C}$ terminal temperature rules.
  • Art. 430.250 & Table 430.52: Motor full-load currents and branch-circuit short-circuit protection (Inverse time breakers at 250%).

📐 Electrical Formulas Reference

1. Single-Phase Voltage Drop ($V_d$):
Vd = (2 × K × I × L) / Cmil
2. Three-Phase Voltage Drop ($V_d$):
Vd = (1.732 × K × I × L) / Cmil
3. Ohm's Law & Power:
I = E / R | P = E × I
4. Three-Phase Power:
kVA = (1.732 × E × I) / 1000

Utah DOPL Electrical Licensing Requirements

  • Journeyman Electrician: Requires 4 years (8,000 hours) of approved apprenticeship experience and 576 classroom hours under a licensed Master Electrician, plus passing the state exam.
  • Master Electrician: Requires 2 years of active licensed Journeyman experience.
  • Continuing Education: 16 hours required every 2-year licensing cycle (renewing Nov 30 of odd years), with at least 12 hours in Core NEC code updates.
